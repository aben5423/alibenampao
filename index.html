01 / ABOUT ME

Technical thinking.
Marketing execution.

I'm Aliben Ampao, a Marketing Technology & Automation Specialist focused on building and managing the systems that support digital businesses.

I work across the connections between CRM, marketing automation, payments, sales funnels, membership platforms, analytics, websites, and infrastructure, making sure these systems work together reliably.

My hands-on experience includes Keap/Infusionsoft, Stripe, Zapier, ClickFunnels, Kajabi, WordPress, Cloudflare, Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics, along with webinar, scheduling, design, and business platforms.

A major part of my work involves building and troubleshooting automated workflows, integrating platforms, managing payment and subscription systems, configuring funnels and membership environments, and solving technical issues when things don't work as expected.

I enjoy finding practical ways to automate repetitive processes, connect disconnected systems, improve workflows, and make digital operations more efficient.
