01 / ABOUT ME

Technical thinking.
Marketing execution.

I'm Aliben Ampao, a Marketing Technology & Automation Specialist with hands-on experience managing the systems and technology that support digital businesses.

My work goes beyond managing individual platforms. I work with the connections between them, from CRM and email automation to payment processing, sales funnels, membership platforms, analytics, websites, and server infrastructure.

I have hands-on experience with Keap/Infusionsoft, Stripe, Zapier, ClickFunnels, Kajabi, WordPress, Cloudflare, Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics, as well as various webinar, scheduling, design, and business tools.

A major part of my work involves building and troubleshooting automated workflows, integrating different platforms, managing payment and subscription systems, configuring funnels and membership environments, and making sure the different parts of a digital business work together properly.

I also have experience working with the technical side of web infrastructure, including hosting, DNS, Cloudflare, SSH, server administration, backups, and monitoring. This allows me to approach digital systems from both a marketing and technical perspective.

What I enjoy most is solving technical problems and finding practical ways to automate repetitive processes, connect disconnected systems, improve workflows, and make digital operations more efficient.
